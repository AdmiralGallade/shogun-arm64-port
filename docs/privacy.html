Shogun 64 privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Shogun 64 collects no personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, automatic crash reporting or purchases, and it does not ask Android for permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere by itself. The only thing that ever reaches the developer is a bug report you choose to email (see below).

What stays on your phone

The game keeps these files in its private storage on your device:

  • your progress, high scores and unlocked missions
  • your settings (controls, sound, difficulty and cheats)
  • a leaderboard nickname, if you type one. The online leaderboard is switched off, so the nickname is never sent anywhere.
  • custom levels you choose to open or share to the game

Uninstalling the app deletes all of them.

Bug reports you send

The "Report a bug" button (Options > Cheats) opens your own email app with a message to the developer already written. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your email app, and you can read, change or delete any of it first. The message contains:

  • your phone's model and Android version
  • the game's version and the mission you were on
  • a log of the current game session, attached as a text file. It records what the game was doing (loading, frame timing, errors) and nothing you typed.
  • your email address and anything else your email app adds, as with any email you send

The developer uses a report only to find and fix the problem it describes, does not share it with anyone, and deletes it once the problem is fixed. To have a report deleted sooner, reply to it and ask.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy your saves and settings to your Google account so they come back on a new phone. Google runs that backup under its own privacy policy, and the developer cannot see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Links out of the game

The "Source on GitHub" button opens the project's page in your web browser. What happens there is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone of any age.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data (for example, if the online leaderboard is switched on), this page will say exactly what and why before that update ships, and the date above will change.
